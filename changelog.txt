Warlockery 1.1.0
================

- Updated 3D models and textures for creatures, machines, ritual blocks, statues, equipment, and decorative blocks.
- Replaced placeholder item art with distinct pixel art, corrected spawn eggs, and improved the creative inventory presentation.
- Reworked the in-game manuals with a readable layout, progression-aware volumes, searchable articles, ritual diagrams, and brew ingredients.
- Improved altars, chalk circles, doll shelves, and ritual displays with clearer interactions, rendered attachments, connected glyphs, and less UI clutter.
- Expanded dolls with durability-based use, self-binding, shelf activation, restricted storage, and scheduled mending behavior.
- Added distinct brew colors, restored missing brew presentation, improved custom brew validation, and added the murderous flock brew.
- Improved creature models and behavior, including clothed Lycan and Kobold villagers, supernatural trading rules, and distinct flying creatures.
- Removed obsolete developer items and redundant blocks, including the separate silver repeater in favor of vanilla crossbow compatibility.
- Fixed altar breaking crashes, missing model bottoms, terrain x-ray gaps, item names, machine inventories, and several progression issues.
- Expanded automated coverage for models, textures, manuals, brews, creatures, rituals, inventories, and multiblock interactions.

Warlockery 1.0.0
================

- First stable public release for Forge 65.1.0, Minecraft 26.2, and Java 25.
- Includes 101 data-driven rituals with interactive chalk-circle selection and requirement displays.
- Includes fixed and custom brewing, ritual machines, altar power, dolls, hexes, vampires, and werewolves.
- Includes 47 creature types, kobold village integration, biome-aware Ents, and silver-equipped Werewolf Hunters.
- Includes Forge automation capabilities, common material tags, searchable in-game manuals, and original pixel art.
- Adds optional JEI recipe-viewer integration when a compatible Forge JEI build is installed.
- Adds English, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Polish, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese for Taiwan.
- Supports texture, model, and sound replacement through standard Minecraft resource packs.
- Adds server configuration for rare world events and Forge update-check metadata.
