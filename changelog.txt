Warlockery 1.5.1-LlaGuiT0-26.2.0.45
=====================================

- Supporter compatibility build compiled and tested specifically for NeoForge 26.2.0.45-beta.
- Uses the exact NeoForge dependency range `[26.2.0.45-beta,26.2.0.46-beta)`.
- Includes the complete Warlockery 1.5.1 feature set listed below.

Warlockery 1.5.1
================

- Isolated Forge GameTests with owned fixtures and scoped environments so neighboring tests cannot leak world state or settings.
- Reworked vampire sustenance so blood replaces hunger, added a dedicated blood-pool HUD, and introduced Sanguine regeneration for a full reserve.
- Made vampire sunlight a semantic damage type that bypasses Fire Resistance while respecting head cover, wetness, and sunless dimensions.
- Corrected vampire death wards so sunlight remains lethal and ordinary lethal hits still land and knock back while leaving one real half-heart, without absorption hearts.
- Added werewolf prey drive in wolf and wolfman forms, with tagged prey, bounded pursuit, and safety-based cancellation.
- Rebuilt the creature roster around readable Minecraft silhouettes and coherent material palettes, including native cat and frog rigs, penguin-like goblin clans, a feminine Banshee, distinct vampires, a human Blood Thrall, an elderly Hedge Crone, a robed Death, a compact Storm Simian, and a gray werewolf.
- Added synchronized third-person wolf and upright werewolf player forms with dedicated first-person forelimb rendering.
- Rebuilt Mandrake and Dreamroot with dedicated creature models, authored geometry, and matching texture atlases.
- Redrew magical plants and rebuilt their crossed-plant models, including distinct hanging Glintweed, occupied Grassper, and armed Plantmine states.

Warlockery 1.5.0
================

- Added dedicated behavior systems across Warlockery's creature families, with bounded perception, navigation, hazard escape, persistence, and lifecycle handling.
- Reworked illusion creatures and the Glass Doppelganger around attributed damage, controlled mimicry, recognition, privacy-safe presentation, and deterministic cancellation.
- Reimagined machine interfaces for clearer slot roles, readable status and power displays, and distinct layouts inspired by classic machine-focused mods.
- Restored and modernized Witchery machine behavior, including continuously powered spinning and distilling, dedicated jar handling, Brazier burn effects, migration-safe inventories, and initiating-brewer ownership.
- Expanded Nami and Naamah's ocean progression, making it clear to bring Nami to an Ocean Monument and defeat its Elder Guardians.
- Added extensive bounded-query and read-budget safeguards to reduce continuous entity and block scanning.
- Updated the normal builds to Forge 65.1.2, NeoForge 26.2.0.64, Fabric Loader 0.19.3, Fabric API 0.158.0+26.2, and stable Fabric Loom 1.17.19; this supporter artifact remains pinned to NeoForge 26.2.0.45-beta.
- Added the NeoForge-only `1.5.0-LlaGuiT0-26.2.0.45` supporter build for LlaGuiT0's modpack, with the exact NeoForge range `[26.2.0.45-beta,26.2.0.46-beta)`.

Warlockery 1.0.0
================

- First stable public release for Forge 65.1.0, Minecraft 26.2, and Java 25.
- Includes 101 data-driven rituals with interactive chalk-circle selection and requirement displays.
- Includes fixed and custom brewing, ritual machines, altar power, dolls, hexes, vampires, and werewolves.
- Includes 47 creature types, goblin village integration, biome-aware Ents, and silver-equipped Werewolf Hunters.
- Includes Forge automation capabilities, common material tags, searchable in-game manuals, and original pixel art.
- Adds optional JEI recipe-viewer integration when a compatible Forge JEI build is installed.
- Adds English, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Polish, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese for Taiwan.
- Supports texture, model, and sound replacement through standard Minecraft resource packs.
- Adds server configuration for rare world events and Forge update-check metadata.
